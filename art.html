The artist

Why a trader paints.

Traders spend their lives reading shapes: the curve of a cup and handle, the three peaks of a head and shoulders, the floor a market tests again and again. TA_ndinsky paints those shapes, together with the moments in history that made them.

The style is Kandinsky-inspired geometric abstraction. The concepts, the hidden signals and every final decision are the artist's own; AI is used as the brush. Each work has a full creation record.

“Non-traders see a painting. Traders see the pattern. The rest is finding the signal.”
