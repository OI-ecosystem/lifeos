TA_ndinsky · Series One · 33 works
Trader meets Kandinsky.

Art made from the patterns traders trade.

Candlesticks, breakouts, crashes and the human moments behind them, in bold geometric abstraction. Every work hides a signal. Look closely.

TA is technical analysis: the trader’s craft of reading shape in a chart. The name is a nod to Wassily Kandinsky, whose geometric abstraction inspired the series.

“Non-traders see a painting. Traders see the pattern. The rest is finding the signal.”
